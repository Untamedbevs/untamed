The Brand at a Glance

Untamed Beverages makes premium ready-to-serve vodka martinis. Each 12 oz can is 15% ALC/VOL and holds two full martinis. Founded by three couples around a backyard fire pit, the brand delivers bar-quality cocktails without the friction or the pretension. The line is four big cats — each a personality, a flavor, and a color. Customers don't just pick a drink, they pick a cat.

Photography & Visual Style
